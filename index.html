Your Learning Pathway

01 · The Basics

What is a Balanced Scorecard?

A strategy evaluation tool used to measure how well an organisation is performing.

It is called "balanced" because it does not only look at money or profit. It also looks at customers, internal operations, and employee learning.
In simple words: The Balanced Scorecard helps an organisation check whether its strategy is working — from different angles, not just one.
02 · Purpose

Why do organisations use it?

Because profit alone does not tell the full story.

  • 📏 Measure performance clearly
  • ✅ Check if strategy is successful
  • 🔍 Identify weaknesses
  • 🧠 Improve decision-making
  • ⚖️ Balance financial & non-financial
  • 🔗 Link daily work to long-term goals
Example — A Bookstore
A bookstore cannot only ask "Did we make profit?" It should also ask:
Are customers satisfied? · Are our operations efficient? · Are our staff improving? · Are we ready for digital competition?

06 · Do It Yourself

Assignment, Step by Step

1
Choose one organisation — POPULAR, MPH, Kinokuniya, Pustaka Mukmin, or any suitable company approved by your lecturer.
2
Describe the company briefly — what it does, who its customers are, and what challenge it faces.
e.g. MPH faces challenges from online platforms, changing reading habits, and fewer walk-in customers.
3
Identify one main strategy — e.g. MPH wants to strengthen online sales and improve customer loyalty.
4
Create a Balanced Scorecard — complete all four perspectives, each with objective, KPI, target and initiative.
5
Explain the scorecard — say why each objective and initiative is suitable.
✍️ Writing Template — Explanation Pattern
"For the financial perspective, the objective is ______. This is important because ______. The KPI used is ______, and the target is ______. The initiative suggested is ______."
Repeat this for all four perspectives, then close with a conclusion: the BSC evaluates strategy in a balanced way — not only profit, but also customers, operations and learning.
07 · Get It Right

Common Mistakes & Good vs Weak

Avoid these traps before you submit.

MistakeWhy it's wrong
Only focusing on profitBSC must cover all four perspectives
KPI too vagueA KPI must be measurable
Target missingThe target shows what you want to achieve
Initiative too generalInitiative must explain the actual action
Same idea in every perspectiveEach must focus on a different area
✗ Weak Example (Customer)
Objective: "Make customers happy" · Measure: "Good service" · Target: "Better" · Initiative: "Improve"
Problem: vague, not measurable, no clear action.
✓ Strong Example (Customer)
Objective: "Improve customer satisfaction" · Measure: "Customer rating" · Target: "Achieve 4.5/5" · Initiative: "Train staff & improve store layout"
Clear KPI, specific target, realistic action.
